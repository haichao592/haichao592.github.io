Biography

Haichao Zhu (朱海潮) is an algorithm engineer working on large language models, retrieval-augmented question answering, agents, and search. His recent work focuses on text model alignment.

At Baidu, he worked in the Search Strategy Department (July 2022 – June 2023) and the Natural Language Processing Department (June 2023 – June 2024), contributing to end-to-end search ranking, ERNIE Bot question answering, reasoning-driven retrieval, and agent evaluation. He was selected for Baidu’s AIDU program.

He earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology in July 2022 under Prof. Bing Qin and previously completed research internships at Microsoft. His research spans reading comprehension, question generation, multimodal understanding, and knowledge distillation.
