Biography

Haichao Zhu works on Natural Language Processing, with research interests in machine comprehension, question generation, knowledge distillation, and text summarization. His research at Harbin Institute of Technology was under the direction of Prof. Bing Qin. He was an intern in the Natural Language Computing Group at Microsoft Research Asia, exploring machine comprehension and text summarization, mentored by Dr. Furu Wei and Dr. Li Dong.

Haichao published research papers in NLP/AI conferences including ACL, EMNLP, and AAAI, and served as a reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLING, AAAI, and IJCAI.
